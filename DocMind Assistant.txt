"DocMind Assistant" — a bilingual (Persian/English) document
question-answering system using a RAG pipeline.

Project structure:
docmind-assistant/
├── app/
│   ├── ui.py           → Streamlit interface
│   ├── config.py       → App-wide settings and constants
│   └── components.py   → Reusable UI components
│
├── core/
│   ├── loader.py       → PDF loading with PyMuPDF
│   ├── chunker.py      → Text chunking strategies
│   ├── embeddings.py   → Embedding generation (Sentence Transformers / API)
│   ├── vector_store.py → FAISS or Chroma vector store management
│   ├── retriever.py    → Semantic retrieval logic
│   ├── qa_chain.py     → LLM-based QA chain (LangChain or LlamaIndex)
│   └── utils.py        → Shared helpers and utilities
│
├── data/
│   ├── raw/            → Uploaded PDFs
│   └── processed/      → Chunked and embedded data
│
├── notebooks/
│   └── experiments.ipynb
│
├── tests/
│   ├── test_loader.py
│   ├── test_chunker.py
│   └── test_retriever.py
│
├── .env.example
├── requirements.txt
└── README.md
